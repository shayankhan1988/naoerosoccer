Partners

Who stands with us

A federation that starts from zero runs on partners who pour their hearts into it. These are ours.

STINGZ

Official kit partner

UK sportswear brand STINGZ designs and makes the Naoero kit, from the first home jersey to the One Child One Ball supporter range. Profits from kit sales flow back into football on the island.

stingz.co

Giordano

Principal sponsor

Fashion brand Giordano is the main sponsor of the national team and supplies home and away kits, goalkeeper shirts, training kits and tracksuits.

giordano.com

PDR

Strategic communications

Dutch agency PDR works with the federation on its strategic communications plan, corporate branding and storyline.

EcoNauru

Climate and education partner

Naoero's largest climate NGO. Its training centre will share a home with the Football Academy & Resilience Hub, combining after-school education with climate awareness.

Naoero Olympic Committee

Parent body

The federation operates under the umbrella of the Naoero Olympic Committee, pulling in the same direction as the island's other sports.
