Privacy

Cookies and privacy

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not use analytics that identify you. It stores nothing on your device.

When you send a message or sign up through a form on this site, the details you enter are stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, and emailed to the federation so we can reply. We use them for that purpose only and do not share them with anyone else. To have your details removed, write to contact@naurusoccer.com.

Links to Facebook, Instagram, X and our kit partner STINGZ lead to those companies' websites, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.
