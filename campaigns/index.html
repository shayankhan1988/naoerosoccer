Campaigns

Everything we raise goes into the game on Naoero. Donations are not yet possible; when they are, you will read it here first.

Flagship campaign

One Child One Ball

Our goal is to give an eco-friendly football to every child on Naoero aged 4 to 14, 3,500 balls in all, by the end of 2026. With every child having access to a ball, we promote early participation and equal access, and we involve every school and community centre on the island.

The balls are made of sustainable materials and carry a message of resilience. With extra equipment, many balls are re-used, so recycling is part of the campaign's success.

You donate through our kit partner STINGZ, who make the balls and ship them to Naoero. Three ways to join in:

£20

Grassroots Supporter. One football for one child on Naoero.

£50

Community Supporter. One football for a child, and the Naoero home shirt for you.

£1,000

Corporate Partner. 100 personalised footballs for a school or district.

Wear the first kit in our history

Naoero's first ever national shirt, made with our kit partner STINGZ and inspired by the flag: ocean blue, the gold equator band and the twelve-pointed star, with a Pacific tribal pattern on the front and back. Home, away and goalkeeper jerseys are available.

Buying the shirt is the most direct way to support football on Naoero: all profits go to the federation's grassroots work. You can buy a shirt with or without donating a ball.
