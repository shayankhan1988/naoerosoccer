Nauru's first ever kit on sale now

3 August 2024 · Nauru Soccer Federation

The Nauru Soccer Federation and Stingz are pleased to unveil the official Nauru jersey for the 2024/25 season.

This piece of history couldn't be rushed

The new home jersey is inspired by Nauru's national flag, coming in a predominantly blue colourway, which represents the Pacific Ocean. There is a gold band across the chest which represents the narrow gold stripe of the flag, and which in turn represents the equator. At the center of the chest band is the 12 pointed white star that also appears on the flag and represents the island nation's twelve original tribes. A Polynesian-inspired tribal pattern is watermarked on the front and back panels to complete the bespoke design.

In terms of construction and design concept, the Nauru home jersey features tonal blue sleeves and golden cuffs and player numbers. It also features a 90's inspired gold and navy deep v-neck collar with contrast gold collar insert construction.

Getting your hands on one... or two?

The new Nauru Soccer home jersey will be available to purchase for £39 (ex shipping) on pre-order early next week along with the official away and GK/3rd jersey. These are player edition, athletic fit jerseys, so be sure to check out the accompanying size chart. The link will be live next week on Stingz.co.

All profits from the shirt sales will go towards supporting the Nauru Soccer Federation and their mission to develop grassroots football on the island nation and continue to use the power of football for positive impact.

In addition, we will be making available 100 limited edition versions of the Nauru home jersey, each individually numbered (1/100 to 100/100), with custom name and number sublimation on the back for £55 (ex shipping). Again, all profits from the shirt sales will go towards supporting the Nauru Soccer Federation.

Technical Director Charlie Pomroy is thrilled with the outcome

"What a beauty! We want to thank Stingz, and everyone else involved, for their hard work and dedication to our project. You're all truly amazing people.

We often hear people saying there is no such thing as a sure thing in football. This is the kind of thinking we as a team will need when we step onto the field of play. We're going to have to be the underdog every time we play and believe we're capable of doing things no one else can imagine. When the players, coaches and fans put on a shirt of this quality it will be a massive boost to our belief we can achieve anything.

The NSF is dedicated to using football for positive impact and whatever we manage to achieve on this mission one thing is for sure... we're going to look very stylish!"
