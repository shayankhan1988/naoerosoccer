Whether you live on Naoero, have Naoeroan roots abroad or simply love the game: there is a place for you.

Represent your country

Are you of Naoeroan descent?

Naoero's first national teams will be built from players on the island and from the diaspora in Australia, Fiji, New Zealand and beyond. If you or your family are from Naoero and you play football at any level, we want to hear from you. Men's, women's and futsal teams are all part of the plan.

Register as a player

What we are looking for

Players

Men and women of Naoeroan descent, anywhere in the world, for the national team pathway.

Children on Naoero

Parents can sign up a child aged 4 to 14 for the grassroots sessions at school or in the community.

Coaches, referees and volunteers

On the island: coaches, teachers, referees-to-be and parent liaisons. Anywhere: writers, designers, fundraisers and people who open doors.

Ambassadors

From every district on Naoero and from the diaspora, to make the federation known and trusted locally.
